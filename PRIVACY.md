# Privacy

CarScreen Play has **no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking**. Nothing is sent to the developers.

| Data | How it is used | Stored | Leaves the phone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Location (GPS)** | Only to calculate speed for the driving lock, while the Android Auto video screen is open (public build), or when you turn the lock on (personal build) | No, only the latest speed is kept in memory | No |
| **Car speed** (if the car provides it) | Second speed source for the driving lock | No | No |
| **Videos on the phone** | Listed and played on request | No copies are made. The app playlist and playback positions are kept in the app's private storage. | No |
| **Websites, YouTube and Google sign-in** | Loaded in the app's WebView. You type your password on Google's page; the app never reads it. | Cookies and site data in the app's private WebView storage. Delete them with *Clear embedded browsing data*. | Only to the websites you open, as in any browser |
| **Video links** | Streamed from the address you enter | In the app playlist (private storage) | Only to that address |
| **Screenshots** | Taken only when you press Screenshot | Your gallery, `Pictures/CarScreen Play` | No |
| **Diagnostics report** | Created only when you export it | Wherever you save it | Only if you share it yourself. URLs, cookies and credentials are excluded. |

Screen mirroring (optional lab feature) captures the screen only after Android's own consent dialog. LAN streaming sends video only to a receiver you pair with an eight-digit code on your local network.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its private data. Screenshots and exported reports stay where you saved them.
